# Beginner gloss cleanup: dad and wish

Released as `curation-2026-10-14a`. Independent review approved on 2026-10-14.
The owner approved the cleanup scope on 2026-10-14.

## Editorial changes

- `bố#1`, batch `0006`: `father; dad (the South says ba)` becomes
  `father; dad`. Keep the `Northern` tag; Southern learners get their own dad term.
- `chúc#1`, batch `0015`:
  `to wish (someone something good); compare cầu (to pray) and ước (to wish in your mind)`
  becomes `to wish (someone something good)`. The short usage note distinguishes
  wishing someone well without introducing untaught related words.

Reviewed all six senses and six examples of bố, and both senses and three examples
of chúc. No other regional comparisons or related-word detours need removal.
Keep the other glosses: their short family-address notes clarify who is speaking;
the humorous and cloth senses of bố and the uncommon downward sense of chúc are
separate meanings, retained under the guide. All examples remain short, useful
illustrations of their respective senses. No sense, example, tag, order, `from`,
base fingerprint or permanent sense ID changes.
