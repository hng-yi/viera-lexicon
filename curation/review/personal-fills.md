## Regional and spelling cautions

Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh (Ho Chi Minh City) is official nationally. Sài Gòn (Saigon)
is colloquial, especially in Southern conversation, but not exclusive to the South.
Singapore / Xin-ga-po (Singapore) are original spelling / transcription, not
North / South variants. Mỹ / Mĩ (the United States), kỹ sư / kĩ sư (engineer),
họa sĩ / hoạ sĩ (painter) and quản lý / quản lí (manager) are orthographic variants,
not dependable regional distinctions. Úc (Australia) is usual everyday speech in
both regions; Ô-xtrây-li-a (Australia) is a formal transcription.

Original spellings and transcriptions of Bangkok, Tokyo, Seoul, Moscow, London
and Phnom Penh differ more by publication style, generation and register than by
region. Do not apply unsupported regional tags. Native confirmation of preferences
and contemporary usage remains pending.
